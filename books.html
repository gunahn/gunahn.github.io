AI Insiders

『AI Insiders』 brings together insights from leading experts at the frontiers of the AI industry and academia. The book showcases voices who are either developing AI at the cutting edge or applying it as a tool to shape the future. Covering breakthroughs in robotics, medicine, drug discovery, and materials science, as well as transformations in creativity, business, and society, it offers one of the most compelling and credible perspectives on how humanity will coexist with AI and the innovations it drives.

Ahn, G. et al. (2025). Smartbooks. ISBN 9791193674307

Wanna Be an Engineer

In an age where the pursuit of STEM education holds immense significance, “Wanna Be an Engineer” emerges as a groundbreaking guide, offering an authentic glimpse into the world of engineering education and careers. The book delves into topics that resonate with any student contemplating a career in STEM — from study methods and life at engineering college to the intricate details of choosing a major and designing a career path.

Ahn, G. et al. (2021). Megastudybooks. ISBN 9791129708168

The Happiest Country in the World, Finland

The book reveals how Finland became the happiest country in the world for six consecutive years in the World Happiness Report (2018–2023). In my quest to understand happiness, I spent 14 months in Finland. Attending a Finnish university and public school and joining a volunteering club taught me the value of trust, human connection, and the intrinsic worth of every individual. Through this book, readers gain insight into what makes Finland a role model for happiness and discover the secrets behind its success in fostering a content, fulfilled society.

Ahn, G. (2020). HarmonyBook. ISBN 9791189930493

AI Python Coding in Easy Words with Autonomous Race Car

This book is a learning resource for the Python programming language. It combines appropriate Python content with insights from artificial intelligence experts, discussing the future implications. Readers delve into Python programming concepts and also gain valuable knowledge about the future from AI specialists, making it a comprehensive guide for both Python and AI enthusiasts.

Kim, C., Kim, Y., Kim, J., Ahn, G. et al. (2022). Hongreung. ISBN 9791156000488

Digital Immigration

“Digital Immigration” is a science fiction novel that imagines a future where we overcome the limitations of human mortality by uploading our neuronal circuitry to a server, enabling us to live forever in a digital utopia termed ‘Digitophia’. It delves into the creation of a new world — not an analog one, but a digital realm where humanity can exist eternally, and where backed-up brain data can interact and thrive as a life form in its own right, marking the dawn of a new era in human evolution and existence.

Ahn, G. et al. (2023). “Digital Immigration”, in Living Ideas of the Future Observer. Porche. ISBN 9791192730905
