Establishing the STEM Vision Scholarship

I’m proud to reflect on my role as the founder of the STEM Vision Scholarship — Korea’s first college student-run scholarship program, providing annual financial aid and mentorship to underrepresented high school students.

30+🚀 STEM members led, publishing “Wanna Be an Engineer” — 20,000+ copies sold
$20K+💰 Initial capital invested so far to kickstart the scholarship fund
$1K⭐ Annual award plus tailored mentorship per under-served student with leadership potential
500+🤝 Students guided toward STEM pathways through vision mentoring

Currently, we provide financial assistance ($1,000) and tailored mentorship to five under-represented high school students annually.
